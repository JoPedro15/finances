# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to foster an open, collaborative, and inclusive environment where everyone is encouraged to participate. We are committed to ensuring that every perspective is genuinely valued and that participation remains a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for all.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Encouraging active participation and ensuring every contributor's opinion is heard and considered.
- Demonstrating empathy, respect, and kindness toward other people.
- Being open to differing viewpoints, technical perspectives, and constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and gracefully apologizing when mistakes are made.
- Avoiding unsolicited financial advice or fraudulent claims about the software's performance.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Dismissing, belittling, or ignoring input and opinions from team members or contributors.
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (physical address, email, etc.) without explicit permission.
- Attempting to inject code that exfiltrates user secrets or sensitive financial data.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces (e.g. using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as a representative at an event).

## Reporting & Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the project maintainer **João Pedro** ([@JoPedro15](https://github.com/JoPedro15)). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Enforcement actions may include a written warning, a temporary ban from the project spaces, or a permanent ban, depending on the severity and history of the behavior.
